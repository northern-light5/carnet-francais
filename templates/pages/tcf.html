The TCF in a nutshell

The Test de connaissance du français is France Éducation international's official French test. It places you on the CEFR scale from A1 to C2 and is used for university admission, French nationality, and immigration to Canada and Québec. Always check the official website and your test centre for the current format and requirements.

Five tips for exam day

  1. Read the question first. Know what to listen for before the audio starts.
  2. Never leave a blank. Eliminate two options and make your best guess.
  3. Watch the clock. Mock exams teach you your natural pace.
  4. Use connectors. Cependant, en revanche, par conséquent lift your writing score.
  5. Speak, don't recite. Examiners reward natural interaction over memorised text.
